# EC145-Rotorcraft-Aerodynamics-Flight-Performance
Airbus Helicopters EC145 / BK117 C-2: Rotor Aerodynamics, Stability & Flight Performance FrameworkAn end-to-end computational flight mechanics framework developed in MATLAB for the Airbus Helicopters EC145 (Kawasaki BK117 C-2) twin-engine light utility helicopter. The suite integrates:Weight & Balance & Center of Gravity (CG) Envelope Verification across 6 operational loading configurations.2D/3D Blade Element Momentum (BEM) Aerodynamic Solver with cyclic pitch/flapping dynamics and non-linear induced inflow iterative relaxation across 4 flight conditions.Comprehensive Flight Performance & Power Balance Engine evaluating level forward flight, vertical climb capabilities, autorotation sink rates, and ground effect (IGE/OGE) up to service ceiling altitudes ($H = 0 \text{ to } 6000\text{ m}$).Technical Specifications & Reference HelicopterThe reference parameters modeled reflect the EC145 / BK117 C-2 certification baseline powered by two Turbomeca Arriel 1E2 turboshaft engines:ParameterSymbolNominal ValueUnitMaximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW)$m_{MTOW}$$3585$$\text{kg}$Empty Weight$m_{empty}$$1792$$\text{kg}$Main Rotor Radius$R_B$$5.50$$\text{m}$Blade Mean Chord$c_b$$0.32$ (Perf) / $0.30$ (BEM)$\text{m}$Rotor Rotational Speed$N_R$$383 - 395$$\text{RPM}$Number of Main Rotor Blades$N_b$$4$$-$Rotor Mast Longitudinal Station$x_{mast}$$4.50$$\text{m}$Installed Max Continuous Power (MCP, SL)$P_{0,max}$$1032$$\text{kW}$Equivalent Parasite Drag Area$f_A$$1.20$$\text{m}^2$Repository Structure├── ec145_weight_and_balance.m      # Module 1: Longitudinal CG envelope & loading scenarios

Weight & Balance: Longitudinal Center of Gravity EnvelopeFormulationThe overall center of gravity position is determined by structural moment summation relative to the forward datum:$$x_{CG} = \frac{M_{total}}{m_{total}} = \frac{M_{empty} + \sum_{i} m_i \cdot x_i}{m_{empty} + \sum_{i} m_i}$$The certified flight envelope is bounded by four critical vertices defined in the official Flight Manual:Point A: $(4.377\text{ m}, 3585\text{ kg})$Point B: $(4.544\text{ m}, 3585\text{ kg})$Point C: $(4.667\text{ m}, 1750\text{ kg})$Point D: $(4.337\text{ m}, 2000\text{ kg})$Operational Loading ScenariosSix critical mission configurations were evaluated to ensure compliance within structural margins:Scenario IDPayload ConfigurationFuel StateMass [kg]CG Position [m]In-Envelope StatusMax P, Max F8 Pax + Pilot + Baggage$100\%$ ($640\text{ kg}$)$3585$$4.434$COMPLIANT50% P, Max F4 Pax + Pilot + Baggage$100\%$ ($640\text{ kg}$)$3260$$4.474$COMPLIANT0P, Max FPilot Only + Baggage$100\%$ ($640\text{ kg}$)$2940$$4.534$COMPLIANTMax P, 0F8 Pax + Pilot + BaggageReserve ($0\text{ kg}$)$2940$$4.386$COMPLIANT50% P, 0F4 Pax + Pilot + BaggageReserve ($0\text{ kg}$)$2620$$4.441$COMPLIANT0P, 0FPilot Only + BaggageReserve ($0\text{ kg}$)$2300$$4.516$COMPLIANT2. Blade Element Momentum (BEM) Rotor AerodynamicsThe aerodynamic solver couples blade element mechanics with 2D momentum conservation across radial ($r$) and azimuthal ($\psi$) coordinates ($61 \times 20$ discretization grid).Theoretical FrameworkKinematics & Flapping Dynamics:
$$\beta(\psi) = C + A\cos\psi + B\sin\psi$$$$\theta(r) = \theta_0 - \theta_{tw} \cdot r$$Local Velocity Decomposition:
$$V_0 = V_i + V_c + U \sin\alpha_T$$$$V_2 = \Omega r + U \cos\alpha_T \sin\psi$$$$V_1 = \sqrt{V_0^2 + V_2^2}, \quad \phi = \arctan\left(\frac{V_0}{V_2}\right)$$Effective Angle of Attack & Elementary Forces:
$$\alpha_{eff} = \beta + \theta - \phi$$$$C_L = C_{L0} + C_{L\alpha}\alpha_{eff}, \quad C_D = C_{D0} + \frac{C_L^2}{\pi \lambda}$$$$dT = dL \cos\phi - dD \sin\phi$$$$dQ = r \cdot (-dL \sin\phi + dD \cos\phi)$$Relaxed Non-Linear Inflow Iteration:
$$V_{i,new} = \frac{dT}{4\pi \rho r \max(V_0, 10^{-3}) dr}$$$$V_i^{(k+1)} = 0.9 V_i^{(k)} + 0.1 V_{i,new} \quad \text{until } \vert{}V_{i,new} - V_i^{(k)}\vert{} < 10^{-2}$$Evaluated Flight RegimesRegime 1: Hover (Punct Fix) — Symmetric downwash distribution, zero advance ratio ($\mu = 0$).Regime 2: Axial Climb (Urcare) — Pure vertical inflow addition ($V_c = 5\text{ m/s}$).Regime 3: Forward Flight (Zbor Orizontal) — Asymmetric advancing/retreating blade aerodynamics ($U = 10\text{ m/s}, \alpha_T = 5^\circ$).Regime 4: Lateral Control (Comanda Laterala) — Lateral cyclic control coupling ($B = 2^\circ, \alpha_T = 2^\circ$).3. Power Balance, Flight Envelopes & Mission PerformanceThe performance module evaluates the global power equilibrium across altitudes from Sea Level ($0\text{ m}$) up to $6000\text{ m}$:Aerodynamic Power EquationsThe total power required by the rotorcraft is decomposed into three primary components:$$P_{req} = (1 + \epsilon_{loss})\left(P_i + P_0 + P_{par}\right)$$Induced Power ($P_i$):
$$P_i = k_f \lambda_i C_T \cdot \left(\rho \pi R_B^2 V_T^3\right)$$
Where non-dimensional inflow $\lambda_i$ is computed by solving Glauert's quartic equation via numeric polynomial root solving:
$$\lambda^4 + \left(\frac{V}{V_T \sqrt{C_T / 2}}\right)^2 \lambda^2 - 1 = 0$$Profile Power ($P_0$):
$$P_0 = \frac{\sigma C_{D0}}{8}\left(1 + K \mu^2\right) \cdot \left(\rho \pi R_B^2 V_T^3\right)$$Parasite Power ($P_{par}$):
$$P_{par} = \frac{1}{2} \rho f_A V^3$$Engine Power Lapse Model (ISA Atmosphere)Turboshaft power degradation with density ratio is modeled according to the empirical Turbomeca Arriel 1E2 characteristic:$$P_{av}(H) = P_{0,max}\left(1.11 \frac{\rho(H)}{\rho_0} - 0.11\right), \quad \rho(H) = \rho_0 \frac{20000 - H}{20000 + H}$$Ground Effect (IGE vs. OGE)The Cheeseman-Bennett proximity model calculates power reduction near terrain:$$\xi_G = 1 - \frac{1}{2\left(1 + 4(H_G / R_B)^2\right)}$$Performance Summary TableAltitude H [m]Pav​ [kW]Vmin​ [km/h]Vopt​ [km/h]Vmax​ [km/h]wc,max​ [m/s]0 (SL)$1032.0$$0.00$$133.20$$268.45$$10.82$1000$921.4$$0.00$$136.80$$257.30$$8.95$2000$818.1$$0.00$$140.40$$244.75$$7.12$3000$721.4$$38.20$$144.00$$230.10$$5.30$4000$630.7$$55.40$$147.60$$212.80$$3.51$5000$545.6$$76.80$$151.20$$190.90$$1.72$6000$465.5$$112.50$$154.80$$158.20$$0.15$(Service ceiling is reached where vertical rate of climb drops below $0.5\text{ m/s}$ around $5800\text{ m}$).Gallery of Verification FiguresPower Required vs. AvailableFlight Ceiling Envelope (V−H)Vertical Rate of ClimbAutorotation Descent RateGround Effect Power Drop (IGE)Horizontal AccelerationExecution GuidePrerequisitesMATLAB R2020b or later.No specialized toolboxes required (written with base MATLAB numerical functions).Running the ScriptsWeight & Balance Envelope:run('ec145_weight_and_balance.m')
